# Community conduct

Participation in this project should be professional, constructive, and welcoming.

Contributors must:

- Discuss tools, evidence, and editorial decisions without personal attacks.
- Respect differences in discipline, career stage, geography, language, and technical background.
- Disclose relevant developer, commercial, or institutional relationships.
- Accept that catalogue inclusion is not endorsement.
- Avoid using contribution channels for advertising or repeated promotional claims.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that is abusive, discriminatory, deceptive, promotional, or unrelated to the catalogue. Serious concerns can be reported privately to the project contact listed in `config/site.yml` once one is added.
