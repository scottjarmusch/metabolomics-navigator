# Education review — 27 October 2026

All 83 catalogue resources were checked against their current source. Eleven written tutorials/training hubs received a content-and-scope review; 71 recordings have verified provider metadata and topic associations; one unavailable recording is archived. The public directory recommends 82 resources. Raw data retains the archived record for provenance.

## Review scope

Written resources: reviewed the linked content, official index or underlying training source, its relationship to the linked tools, and version/currency limitations. Tutorial code, external services and exercises were not executed. The MetFrag presentation loads its Markdown training source dynamically; the underlying official README was inspected rather than treating its empty HTML shell as the lesson.

Recordings: YouTube's public oEmbed metadata confirmed the video title and uploading provider. GNPS and mzmine workshop indexes were checked for contextual topic associations. This is recorded as `source_checked`, not a complete instructional-content review. Videos were not watched end to end; provider metadata does not guarantee playback for every location/account or certify pedagogical or scientific quality. Keynotes without explicit topics retain general descriptions and no inferred tool associations. Earlier publication/event dates were retained unless a date was independently rechecked.

The page distinguishes “Reviewed”, “Source checked” and “Needs review”. This replaces the blanket unreviewed display with the actual level of evidence, without claiming full-video review.

## Corrections

- The Spectrum Resolver workshop covers spectrum retrieval/rendering with Universal Spectrum Identifiers. It is distinct from GNPS2 Spectrum Summary; its incorrect link to that tool is replaced with the parent GNPS association.
- GNPS Dashboard's teaching example is by Michael T. Marty. VMOL mzmine recordings are uploaded by Functional Metabolomics Lab, rather than EMBL-EBI. Portuguese GNPS community recordings retain their actual uploading providers.
- The first VMOL mzmine recording is a non-target LC-MS/MS processing workshop, not merely an introduction to LC-MS. Library-generation training explicitly covers MSn. Version-specific MZmine 2/3 and MetaboAnalyst material is qualified.
- MassQL in MS-DIAL 5 is a silent demonstration; “no audio” is explicit in its title and description.
- The MS-FINDER written tutorial states it was last edited in December 2018. Its updated year and legacy-compatibility note are recorded.
- The recording `Cc6eKGZQ1Qw` returns HTTP 401 from YouTube's metadata service. It remains unreviewed and is archived, not relabelled as validated or silently deleted. No replacement recording identity is inferred.
