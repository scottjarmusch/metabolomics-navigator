# Alpha reviewer checklist

Ask Navigator: https://scottjarmusch.github.io/metabolomics-navigator-alpha/ask/

Education: https://scottjarmusch.github.io/metabolomics-navigator-alpha/education/

Please start with a real question in your own words. Do not assume the software has checked whether a suggested method is compatible with your experiment.

For each attempt, record your question, experience level, study stage, measurements available, what you expected, what appeared, and whether you knew what to do next. Do not include patient identifiers or confidential study data.

1. Plan a study before collecting samples. Are the clarifying questions understandable to a newcomer?
2. Bring raw data or a feature table. Is the next step clear, and are missing requirements visible?
3. Ask about a named tool. Does its catalogue description answer your question, or is more guidance needed?
4. Try a question with missing QC injections, MS/MS spectra or tracer measurements. Does the response avoid implying that the unavailable information can simply be recovered?
5. Follow a Strategy's publication, workflow and tool links. Are the actual published steps distinguishable from possible alternatives?
6. Find a relevant Education recording. Is the description specific enough to decide whether to watch it? Does the tool/version shown agree with the card? Report the recording URL and timestamp for discrepancies.

Publication year is not a guarantee of current software instructions. Education entries marked Needs review have not been watched end to end. Review feedback should distinguish factual errors, unclear wording, missing content and usability problems.

Release gates: resolve misleading recommendations and broken interactions; review workflow compatibility and scientific scope; obtain novice feedback before promoting Ask Navigator. Keep bulk intake and unrelated held updates out of the release.
