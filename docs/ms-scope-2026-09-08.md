# MS-only scope update — 8 September 2026

The current catalogue focuses on mass-spectrometry-based metabolomics. Removed the two NMR-only tool entries, **nmrglue** and **NMRProcFlow**, from the active catalogue and task guide. Their previous records remain recoverable in Git history. No existing strategy was NMR-only: all 13 remain in scope.

Retained MetaboLights, Metabolomics Workbench and Workflow4Metabolomics for their MS use. Removed NMR-specific platform/input tags and focused their entry prose on MS workflows. Original publication titles are unchanged, even where they mention NMR.

Removed NMR and infrared/Raman platform choices, NMR spectra and nmrML vocabulary values, and the corresponding schema/form options. Shared platform-independent resources still require demonstrated relevance to MS metabolomics. Updated the guide introduction, site description and contribution policy.

The catalogue now contains **79 tools and resources and 13 strategies**. Earlier expansion and publication-audit reports retain their historical counts and decisions.
