# Spatial and structural-lipid Strategy review

- Epoxidation: traced the 2018 phospholipid paper back to its 2017 fatty-acid precursor (10.1021/acs.analchem.7b01870). The earlier paper is primary and 2018 is an extension. Distinct from oxetane photochemistry and ozonolysis. No claim that ordinary lipid data support positional assignments.
- Tissue iso-imaging: 2022 DOI 10.1038/s41592-021-01378-y formalizes tracer infusion, MALDI isotope images, LC-MS validation/normalization and IsoScope. Keep the card specific to this implementation, not the invention of every isotope-imaging approach. Not equivalent to absolute flux or universally single-cell resolution.
- 13C-SpaceM: 2024 DOI 10.1038/s42255-024-01118-4 remains under review as a potential extension or distinct integration of tracer, MALDI all-ion fragmentation and cell registration. Do not turn the cancer application alone into a Strategy.

Primary bibliographic identities checked using publisher/PubMed records and Crossref. Experimental workflows not independently executed. Requirements and negative cases are included for Ask compatibility review.
