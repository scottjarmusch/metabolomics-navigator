# Ask Navigator reviewer checklist

Preview: https://scottjarmusch.github.io/metabolomics-navigator-alpha/ask/

Education is now public: https://scottjarmusch.github.io/metabolomics-navigator/education/

Start with your own research question, especially if you are new to metabolomics. Record the exact question, study stage, available measurements, expected next action, actual response and whether the next step is understandable. Do not include identifying or confidential data.

1. Try planning a study before collecting data. Are the scientific aim and measurement questions understandable? Does the response help you approach your MS facility?
2. Try raw instrument files, a processed feature table, and uncertainty about what you have. Can you reach relevant processing/QC explanations and tutorials?
3. Try missing fragmentation, QC or tracer measurements. Does the response explain the missing requirement without recommending an unsuitable method?
4. Ask about LC-MS2Struct, ROASMI or MS2DECIDE. Can you distinguish the original Strategy, later implementations and their software?
5. Follow a publication, requirements list, workflow tool and Education link. Report broken links and unclear or unsupported claims.
6. Try a question with no relevant match. Can you still explore the catalogue or clarify your question?
7. Change your answers and use Start over. Check that old recommendations and guidance disappear.

Submit feedback through the existing GitHub issue route: https://github.com/scottjarmusch/metabolomics-navigator/issues/new

For each finding, identify factual accuracy, compatibility, wording or interaction. Screenshots and expected behavior are useful. A matched publication does not establish suitability for a particular study; generic alternatives are exploration links. Education resources retain their individual review and date labels.

Before production: fix misleading recommendations and interaction failures; obtain novice and scientific feedback; evaluate measurement/input/output compatibility for alternatives. The present automated evaluation is recorded in `docs/ask-evaluation-2026-10-07.md` and does not replace that feedback.
