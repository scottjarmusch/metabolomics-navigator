# Publication-scope audit — 8 September 2026

Reviewed all 81 tool entries and 13 strategy entries present at commit `304f56216d7d136894b3d785663983a84f9fc640`. This is a scope/attribution audit, not a systematic search for every historical release paper, a citation-impact assessment, or software testing. Existing maintenance/developer-verification dates were preserved.

Tool publication records: 91 before; 9 removed for scope/attribution; 10 reclassified as software/documentation citations; 7 verified scholarly replacements added; 79 after. DOI links were resolved for HMDB, MS2LDA, mummichog and patRoon. Retained citation text was checked against Crossref/Europe PMC metadata; titles and abstracts informed resource identity. Preprints remain explicitly labelled and are not represented as peer reviewed. This audit does not establish that a newer journal version is unavailable.

## Important attribution decisions

- **MZmine:** retained the MZmine 3 introduction, its Nature Protocols procedures and the MZmine 2 lipid-annotation extension. Removed the application-specific lipid A workflow and the separate MSnLib resource. The lipid A paper does include software extensions; exclusion reflects this narrower bibliography, not an absence of methods development.
- **SIRIUS:** component papers remain on the CSI:FingerID, CANOPUS and MSNovelist tool pages and the ZODIAC/COSMIC strategy pages. Parent/component links remain intact.
- **MASST/FASST:** the [Mongia paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-023-01985-4) introduces MASST+ and Networking+. It was removed from both original MASST and FASST. The [microbeMASST methods and references](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-023-01575-9) link FASST to P-massive (Batsoyol et al., 2022); that backend paper was not substituted as a dedicated FASST paper without a clearer scope match.
- **MS-DIAL:** “A lipidome atlas in MS-DIAL 4” explicitly introduces MS-DIAL 4, so it qualifies as a direct update.
- **GNPS:** FBMN and IIMN are methods implemented in GNPS, so those papers qualify, alongside their dedicated strategy pages.
- **msconvert:** retained the explicitly labelled ProteoWizard platform citation, following [official citation guidance](https://proteowizard.sourceforge.io/publications.html).
- **MSnLib:** candidate for separate resource review; no new entry was added.
