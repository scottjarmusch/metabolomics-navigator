Outline a workflow from scratch

Use this outline to assemble a plan from your study question and available data. Record the inputs, outputs and requirements for each step, then review how the steps fit together.

  1. Define your starting point. Record your study question, sample groups, available measurements and file formats in the study guide.
  2. Prepare and check the data. Explore processing tools and QC resources. Check that your files, controls and outputs fit.
  3. Select an analytical step. Search the tool catalogue for your aim. Connect a step only when its required inputs are provided by the previous step.
  4. Interpret and review. Record assumptions, identification confidence, software versions and gaps. Review the outline with your analyst before using it.
