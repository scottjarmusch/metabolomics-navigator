HOW THE ALPHA WORKS

Precedent first. Alternatives second.

  1. Your scientific questionWhat you want to accomplish, not a software name.
  2. Published StrategyA transferable method with an originating citation.
  3. Implemented workflow

    Alternative searches use the recorded tool function; check inputs, outputs and acquisition compatibility before substituting.

    The experimental and computational sequence actually reported.
  4. Published applications and extensionsLater papers show where the Strategy has been reused or adapted.
  5. Catalogue alternativesThe full tool catalogue broadens individual workflow steps without rewriting the published precedent.

Ask Navigator does not treat publication frequency, citation counts, or one software ecosystem as proof that a tool is universally preferable. Published implementations are evidence of precedent; alternatives remain study- and compatibility-dependent.
