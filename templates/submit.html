From suggestion to shared resource

  1. Submit

    A GitHub account is needed. New-entry forms create structured drafts for review; update forms open correction issues.

  2. Review

    Editors check scientific scope, source evidence, metadata and neutral wording. They may ask for clarification.

  3. Publish & maintain

    Accepted changes are openly versioned. Editorial review and developer or author verification remain distinct.

Authors, developers and users are welcome. Please disclose any connection to the resource and avoid unsupported comparative claims.
